Dagar Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Dagar is a vocabulary notebook app that runs on your computer. In short: Dagar does not collect, send or share any personal data. Everything you write stays on your computer.

What is stored, and where

Information created while you use Dagar is stored only on your computer, in the app's own folder:

  • the words, meanings, example sentences and notes you add;
  • your review history (which card you answered, when and how);
  • your settings (colour, shortcut, daily goal, reminder time, level).

To protect you from data loss, Dagar also keeps automatic backups in Documents\Dagar\Yedekler. You can change this folder in Settings.

The dictionary and the AI models ship inside the app and run on your computer; nothing you write is given to them anywhere outside your computer.

Internet access

Dagar does not connect to the internet. It does not ask you to create an account, and it sends no ads, analytics, crash reports or usage statistics. It does not transfer data to any third-party service.

Error log

When an error or crash happens, Dagar writes a short line to a small log file on your computer (logs\dagar.log, at most 1 MB, at most two files): the time, the app version and the error message. Your words, notes and review history are not written to it. The log stays on your computer; Dagar never sends it anywhere. If you want to report a problem, you can open the folder from Settings → About → "Show error log" and attach the file to your e-mail yourself.

Permissions the app uses

  • Clipboard (read only): when you open the quick-add window yourself, Dagar reads the text on the clipboard once to fill in the word you copied (or to suggest a meaning from a sentence you copied). The clipboard is not read at any other time, never written to, and what is read is not sent anywhere.
  • Keyboard shortcut: Dagar registers a single shortcut so you can open quick-add from anywhere. It does not listen to or record any other keystrokes.
  • Notifications: if you turn on reminders, Dagar shows a notification at the time you choose. The notification is created on your computer.
  • Opening files: when you import a file, Dagar reads only the file you pick.

Microsoft Store

When you get Dagar from the Microsoft Store, downloading, installing and updating are handled by Microsoft. Any information Microsoft collects in the process is covered by the Microsoft Privacy Statement; Dagar has no access to it.

Deleting and moving your data

  • All your data is on your computer, so deleting it is up to you. Uninstalling the app also deletes the data in the app's folder.
  • Backups in Documents\Dagar\Yedekler are not deleted on uninstall (so you don't lose your data by accident); you can delete that folder yourself.
  • In Settings → Data you can export your notebook as a Dagar backup, CSV or Anki file.

Children

Dagar is suitable for all ages and collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page and the date at the top updated. If Dagar ever adds a feature that uses the internet, it will work only with your explicit consent and be described here first.
